APPLICATIONS / NEUROPSYCHOLOGY

Neurophysiological Data in Neuropsychological Practice

Neuropsychological assessment describes cognitive performance, behavior and everyday functioning. Neuro-Amea adds physiological measurements to that picture.

WHAT WE OBSERVE

Cognitive and Behavioral Observation

Standardized tests, structured observation and clinical interview can provide information about attention, memory, executive functions, language and behavior.

WHAT WE MEASURE

Recorded Physiological Data

Neuro-Amea records DC potentials at defined scalp locations, producing a separate, quantitative layer of physiological information.

ADDITIONAL LAYER

An Additional Layer — Not a Replacement

The physiological data is reviewed alongside standardized neuropsychological measures, not instead of them. A Neuro-Amea recording alone does not establish a cognitive, developmental or psychiatric diagnosis.
