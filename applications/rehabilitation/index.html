TWO KINDS OF CHANGE

Clinical Change and Physiological Change

Clinical and functional change observed through standard outcome measures can be reviewed alongside recorded physiological change captured by Neuro-Amea.

Repeated Neuro-Amea measurements can show whether recorded physiological parameters differ between assessments. They do not, by themselves, establish treatment effectiveness or causality.
