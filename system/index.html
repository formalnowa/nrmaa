The Neuro-Amea Assessment System

A compact system for recording, processing and visualizing DC potentials — bringing together equipment, software, methodology and professional support.

OVERVIEW

More Than Equipment

Neuro-Amea is built as a system rather than a single instrument: a recorder, sensors, software, methodology and professional support work together to turn a physiological signal into information a specialist can use.
